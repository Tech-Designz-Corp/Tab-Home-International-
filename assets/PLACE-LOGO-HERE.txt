PLACE YOUR LOGO HERE
====================

Save the Tabitha's Home International, Inc. logo in THIS folder with the
exact file name:

    Logo.png

That's it. Every page (header, hero, about, footer, favicon) automatically
uses assets/Logo.png.

Until you add it, the site temporarily loads the logo from the original
uploaded location and, if that is unavailable, shows an elegant "T" monogram
so nothing ever looks broken.

For best results use a square PNG with a transparent background.

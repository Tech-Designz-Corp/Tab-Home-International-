Who we are

Official mission and vision

Tabitha's Home International is a Christian ministry. Service availability is confirmed with the organization. It is separate from the statements below.

Our Mission

To empower individuals and families to overcome adversity through holistic, compassionate care that restores dignity, builds resilience, and inspires hope.

Our Vision

To see every vulnerable person empowered to live with hope and dignity, providing them the opportunity to reach their God-given potential.

T
What guides us

Our core values

Compassion

We meet women, children, and families with care that honors their dignity.

Dignity

Every person is worthy of honor and respect.

Faith

The ministry is Christian, rooted in God's love and the hope found in Him.

Restoration

We believe healing and renewal are possible.

Hope

We want people facing hardship to have room for a future.

Service

We look out for what is better for others, unto the least of these.

Independence

Support should strengthen a person's own next steps, when that is what they want.

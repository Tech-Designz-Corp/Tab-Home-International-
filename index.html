Who We Are

A Place of Refuge, Restoration & Renewed Purpose

Tabitha's Home International, Inc. exists to restore, empower, and transform the lives of women and children who have experienced abuse, neglect, exploitation, or hardship. Grounded in Christian faith, we walk alongside each person with compassion and care, helping them become who God created them to be.

Through safe shelter, community-based support, and faith-centered encouragement, we provide more than help — we offer hope, healing, and a path toward independence.

Our Mission

To restore, empower, and transform the lives of women and children who have been violated through any form of abuse and/or exploitation.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of hope and a place of refuge where women and children who have been abused, neglected, or exploited can become who they were created to be.

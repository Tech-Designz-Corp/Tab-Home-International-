Privacy

This notice matches the website as it is written now. It is not a security certification. Use of the site is also described in the terms of service.

What this site is

The public pages are served by Vercel. Member accounts and form submissions are stored in the organization's MySQL database on TiDB Serverless. The API checks TiDB's certificate against the public ISRG Root X1 certificate. There is a sign-in cookie for a member session. The cookie is HttpOnly and is sent only to this site. This site does not use session replay. It does not include an analytics product that records message contents. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Vercel and TiDB keep their own hosting logs under their terms.

Health information and control reports

This website is not a medical-record system. Do not submit health details, diagnoses, treatment notes, insurance numbers, abuse narratives, or children's names. The organization can read stored form fields and member records. A signed-in member can open only the row tied to that member's session. The connection uses HTTPS. This is not end-to-end encryption.

This notice is not a HIPAA certification. The organization has not published a SOC 1 report or a SOC 2 report for this website. Vendor security descriptions from Vercel or TiDB are those companies' documents. They are not an audit of Tabitha's Home International.

Accounts and forms

Membership sign-up stores a name, email, phone, membership type, email-update choice, and a hash of the password. Contact and newsletter forms store the fields on those forms. The organization can read those records. The connection uses HTTPS. This is not end-to-end encryption. Do not include abuse details or children's names. The published contact address is info@tabithashomeintl.org. That address is not described here as a monitored emergency line.

When a form or account is saved, the site may send an automated notice through Resend to the address on the form and to the organization. Resend and the organization can read that message. The notice does not promise a reply, and an email-update request is not a recurring newsletter.

A signed-in member can view that member's own record, change the password, change the name, phone, and email-update choice, or delete the account. Deletion removes the member row and its sessions. Forgotten-password email is not available on this site.

Donations

This site does not collect card numbers or show a payment as complete. Online giving is not open. If a hosted checkout is connected later, the amount, currency, and receipt would be handled on that provider's site.

Launch meeting

The join page opens Zoom in a new tab when a Zoom link is connected. It does not place the meeting inside this site. Visitors are not asked for camera or microphone access. Zoom can receive the fact that someone opened their link.

Stories and photographs

This launch does not publish beneficiary stories or children's photographs. To ask that something be taken down, email info@tabithashomeintl.org and describe the page. Removal still depends on a new publication of the site.

Information guide

The information guide runs in the browser. It does not send what you type to the organization. Leaving the site with that button does not erase browser history.

About Tabitha's Home International, Inc.

A faith-centered ministry restoring hope and rebuilding the lives of women and children through God's love.

Our Story

Helping Women & Children Become Who God Created Them to Be

Tabitha's Home International, Inc. was born from a deep conviction that no woman or child should face abuse, neglect, exploitation, or hardship alone. Inspired by the example of Tabitha — a woman known for her acts of kindness and service — we exist to be a refuge of safety, compassion, and renewal.

We provide more than shelter. We surround each person with dignity, prayer, practical support, and a community that believes in their God-given worth and future.

Our Mission

To restore, empower, and transform the lives of women and children who have been violated through any form of abuse and/or exploitation.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of hope and a place of refuge where women and children who have been abused, neglected, or exploited can become who they were created to be.

Our Purpose in God

To reflect Christ's love through compassion, dignity, and service — caring for the least of these and walking each person toward healing and hope.

"Instead of each person watching out for their own good, watch out for what is better for others."Philippians 2:4

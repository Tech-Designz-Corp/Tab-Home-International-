About Tabitha's Home International

A Christian ministry. The mission and vision on this page are the official statements.

Our story

Named for practical kindness

The ministry takes its name from Tabitha, who was known for practical kindness. It serves women, children, and families facing abuse, hardship, or instability.

Ask the organization which services are open before you rely on a particular program. The broad mission is not a list of openings.

T

Our Mission

To empower individuals and families to overcome adversity through holistic, compassionate care that restores dignity, builds resilience, and inspires hope.

Our Vision

To see every vulnerable person empowered to live with hope and dignity, providing them the opportunity to reach their God-given potential.

Faith

The ministry's Christian identity is explicit. That purpose sits beside the official mission and vision. It does not replace them.
